# 📊 Sales & Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🔹 Overview
This project is a **Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI**.  
It was designed to transform raw business data into **actionable insights**, enabling decision-makers to monitor sales performance, identify trends, and optimize strategies effectively.  

The dashboard provides an **interactive and visual overview** of key sales metrics such as revenue, profit, customer segmentation, and product performance.  

---

## 🔹 Features
- 📈 **Sales Trends**: Visualizations of revenue and profit over time.  
- 🛒 **Product Performance**: Breakdown of sales by product category and top-selling items.  
- 🌍 **Geographic Insights**: Sales distribution across different regions.  
- 👥 **Customer Analysis**: Segmentation of customers by behavior and purchase volume.  
- ⚡ **Dynamic Filters**: Slicers and filters for interactive exploration (date, region, product).  

---

## 🔹 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop (.pbix)**  
- **Data Cleaning & Modeling** with Power Query  
- **DAX Measures** for custom KPIs  
- **Interactive Visualizations** with charts, maps, and cards
